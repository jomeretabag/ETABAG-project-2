About KCC

A community shaped by faith and learning.

Get to know Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., located in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental.

Our identity

Learning, faith, and service

Kabankalan Catholic College brings together a community of learners guided by its Catholic identity. The college seal reflects its home in Kabankalan City and displays 1927 as an important part of its history.

Inspired by the call to “Teach ye all nations,” KCC celebrates education as a path to personal growth and service to others.

Our Purpose

Vision, Mission, & Core Values

Vision

Our shared direction

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

Mission

Our commitment

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Core Values

What guides us

  • Kindness
  • Christ-centeredness
  • Creativity
  • Integrity
  • Accountability
  • Nurturing
  • Synodality
