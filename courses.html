Academic Strands

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics

For students who intend to pursue college degrees in the sciences, engineering, technology, mathematics, and related fields. It focuses on advanced mathematics, sciences, and research.

Key Subjects

  • Pre-Calculus
  • Basic Calculus
  • General Biology
  • General Chemistry
  • General Physics
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Engineering, Medicine, IT/Computer Science, Architecture, and Research

ABM

Accountancy, Business, and Management

Designed for students who plan to pursue business-related courses such as business administration, accountancy, marketing, and entrepreneurship.

Key Subjects

  • Fundamentals of ABM
  • Business Mathematics
  • Business Finance
  • Organization & Management
  • Applied Economics
  • Principles of Marketing

College Paths

Accountancy, Business Administration, Entrepreneurship, Marketing, and Economics

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

For students who wish to take up journalism, communication arts, liberal arts, education, and other social science programs in college.

Key Subjects

  • Creative Writing
  • Creative Nonfiction
  • Introduction to World Religions
  • Trends, Networks, & Critical Thinking
  • Philippine Politics & Governance
  • Community Engagement

College Paths

Education, Law, Psychology, Mass Communication, and Public Administration

GAS

General Academic Strand

A flexible strand for students who are still undecided on their college course. It covers a broad range of subjects from different academic tracks.

Key Subjects

  • Humanities
  • Social Sciences
  • Applied Economics
  • Organization & Management
  • Electives from other strands
  • Research/Capstone

College Paths

Any college program, Liberal Arts, Education, Business, and Social Sciences
